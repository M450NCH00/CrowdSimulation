## 1. Research question

How team members negotiate the **termination** of verbal conflicts at work: the paper
"considers verbal conflicts at the workplace and asks how team members negotiate conflict
termination" (Abstract).

## 2. The categories / scheme

**Three termination formats**, named in the abstract. The abstract gives the names and
their frequency ordering but **not their definitions**:

| Paper's label | Paper's definition | Locator |
|---|---|---|
| **submission** | `[not accessible — definition is in the full text]` | Abstract |
| **stand-off** | `[not accessible]` | Abstract |
| **compromise** | `[not accessible]` | Abstract |

One further operational definition **is** given in the abstract: conflict itself is
defined as **continued disagreement** — "Following the literature, we define conflicts as
continued disagreement" (Abstract).

The abstract also names two further analytic dimensions without defining them: **episode
length** and **disagreement mitigation**, which "indicate the severity of the conflict"
(Abstract).

**The three "patterns of conflict minimization" promised by the subtitle are not
enumerated in the abstract** — `[not accessible]`. The abstract states only that "the
pattern of conflict minimization varies with the termination format that participants
negotiate" (Abstract).
