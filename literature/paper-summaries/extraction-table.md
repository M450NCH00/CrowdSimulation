# Extraction table — the six selected papers

Prepared for issue [#14](https://github.com/M450NCH00/CrowdSimulation/issues/14).

**What this file is.** One row per paper, recording only what each paper says. Category
names are reproduced **verbatim** in each paper's own terminology.

**What this file deliberately does NOT do.** It does not map, align or equate categories
across papers. For example, "obliging" (paper 04), "submission" (paper 05) and
"relinquishing a position" (paper 06) appear in three separate rows with no claim that
they are the same construct. Whether they are is a research judgement requiring supervisor
sign-off, and asserting it here would bake an unverified equivalence into everything
downstream. No conclusions are drawn in this file.

⚠ Papers **05 and 06 were paywalled**; their rows carry only what their abstracts state.
See those files for the accessibility boundary.

## Column notes

- **"Frequency data?"** records whether the paper reports how often something occurred,
  regardless of whether it is about human behaviour or method performance. For Group 1
  papers the numbers are performance metrics, not behaviour base rates.
- **"Validated against human behaviour?"** is answered for Group 1 papers only; for
  Group 2 papers the question does not apply, because their data *are* human behaviour.
- **"Reusable pattern extracted?"** uses this FYP's sense: something applied **without**
  re-running the LLM.
