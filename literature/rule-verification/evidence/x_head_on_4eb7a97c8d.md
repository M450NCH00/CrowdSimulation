# x_head_on_4eb7a97c8d: people signal (eye contact, a nod, waving on) when they can

Issue #69 (RF-14). Sources keyed as in `../sources.md`.

- **Says (RF-11):** in the non-verbal and talk conditions, people signal (eye contact, a nod, waving
  the other on), and speak when the other drifts to their side or the plan fails. Found in 2 of 3 runs;
  predicts `signalled`; tagged needs communication.
- **Fidelity (RF-11):** 40 of 52 held out (0.77) against 0.35; mostly restates the condition, since
  nobody can signal in the independent condition.
- **Simulation:** none. Not expressible in rule format 3 or 4 (no field for signals).

## Evidence

**For (gaze is read and used):** people "infer other people's movement trajectories from their gaze
direction and … use this information to … plan their own movement"; shown a walker looking to one
side, participants passed on the other ([Nummenmaa2009], abstract; animated walker, laboratory). A
look is a signal people use when passing.

**Against (explicit signals between strangers):** between unacquainted passers-by the norm is civil
inattention, "regard without interest … allocated to unacquainted persons without sharing an
invitation to become involved in engagement" ([Arminen2023], full text, introduction, after Goffman);
the study measures gaze lengths on video and counts longer gazes as breaches (abstract). Engagement,
such as a greeting, is for those "ratified to receive focused attention", so a rule that strangers
routinely nod or wave each other on goes beyond the evidence. No study was found that counts nods or waves between strangers meeting head-on.

## Grade: Partly supported

Using the other's gaze to choose a side is supported. Routine explicit signals between strangers are
not: civil inattention says a glance, not a nod. In Step 1 most signalling came from acquaintances
(RF-10: knowing the other, OR 221 for signalling), which fits the evidence better than the rule's
wording does.

## Decision (#55's table)

**Matches (partly), not tested in the simulation** (not expressible). Recorded why: **the format** has
no field for a signal. Stays staged, tagged needs communication, with its grade.
