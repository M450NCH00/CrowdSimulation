## A. Check before submission

### ~~G1 — Zhang et al. [1] full text~~ — ✅ RESOLVED 2026-09-13
- **Was:** the Research Gap sentence said DFD compares its rules with symbolic regression
  "rather than human behaviour". Only the first half was in the abstract, so the sentence
  carried `[needs verification against full text]`. This was blocking.
- **Resolution:** the user supplied the PDF (Just Accepted TOMACS version, now cached as
  `refs/zhang2026_dfd.pdf`). The claim **holds**:
  - all three datasets (train, test, out-of-domain) are generated by the LLM-driven
    simulation, and fidelity is measured against that ground truth (§5.1);
  - §6.1: "As our approach is agnostic of the realism of the ground truth trajectories,
    the validity of the distilled function depends directly on the reference data";
  - §6.2 names distilling from real-world observations (e.g. video) only as "an
    interesting alternative".
- **One nuance the abstract hid:** DFD's ground truth comes from Wu et al.'s simulation,
  which *does* include a communication phase, and agents' conversations are part of the
  textual trajectory data used to generate insights (§4.2). But the decisions distilled
  are exit choices, the final functions take only numerical perception features (§3,
  Fig. 14), and encoding agent interactions as features is future work (§7). The gap
  sentence ("extracts a reusable rule from LLM-to-LLM **conflict dialogue** and validates
  it against how people resolve the same conflict") remains accurate. This nuance is
  recorded in the source map (row G7) rather than spent on words in the draft.
- **Draft changes:** marker removed; sentence rewritten to cite the §5.5 run times and
  the §6.1 validity statement.

### G5 — The research gap is bounded by the review, not by a systematic search
- **What:** "None of the reviewed studies extracts a reusable rule from LLM-to-LLM
  conflict dialogue and validates it against how people resolve the same conflict."
- **Status:** true of the 14 papers in the issue #8 review, and worded that way on
  purpose. It is **not** evidence that no such work exists. Issue #8 was a targeted
  review, and its own `search-log.md` records blocked sources (ACM DL, APS, Springer,
  ScienceDirect).
- **How to close:** a short targeted search for "LLM dialogue" + "distillation" /
  "convention extraction" + "pedestrian validation" before the final report makes a
  stronger novelty claim. This does not block Assignment 1, as long as the wording stays
  limited to the reviewed studies.

## B. Nice to have — claims that lack a source in the current review

Each of these would improve the introduction but lacks a source from the current
literature review, so it was **left out of the draft**.

| # | Claim that would help | Where it would go | What kind of paper would supply it |
|---|---|---|---|
| N1 | Conflicts make **crowd simulations** (not robots) stall or deadlock | Background | A crowd-simulation paper reporting gridlock or deadlock at bottlenecks or in counterflow in agent-based or social-force models.
