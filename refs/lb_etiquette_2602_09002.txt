   Before designing the complete system, we conducted a
user study with 54 participants to evaluate whether a VLM
can reliably select socially compliant paths in various sce-
narios. Each image is annotated with 3 - 4 feasible paths by
human experts (see Fig. 10). Participants were asked to select
one path aligned with their own daily walking preference
while respecting social norms. We compare these selections
with 12 VLM queries per image.
