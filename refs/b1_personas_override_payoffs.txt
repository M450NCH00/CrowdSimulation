3.5 Scenario Construction

We construct 53 scenarios by specifying payoff tables over the 16
joint action profiles and pairing them with natural-language narra-
tives describing the policy context. Scenarios are generated by first
choosing a target equilibrium structure, constructing payoffs con-
sistent with that structure, and then generating narratives aligned
with the incentives. We manually filter scenarios to ensure that the
narrative meaningfully reflects the underlying payoff structure.

select Green Transition actions (socially preferred but payoff-suboptimal) in Tragedy-dominant scenarios where Tragedy of
the Commons is the payoff-optimal equilibrium. With personas present, all models select Green Transition actions at rates of

     short-term profitability with long-term sustainability and corporate responsibility. While the immediate economic pressures favor polluting
