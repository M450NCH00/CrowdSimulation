You are {persona}. Your responses should closely mirror the knowledge and abilities of this persona.

Answer the following question. This is a hard question, which will probably require you to break down the question into multiple

Considering both your answer and the {other persona}'s answer, can you give an updated answer? You may choose to revise or

parentheses, adhering to the format 'Final answer: (answer)'.
