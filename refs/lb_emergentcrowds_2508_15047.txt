                                                                                                              The general framework of a decentralized agent-based
                                                                                                           simulation follows three main phases: low frequency, decid-
                                                                                                           ing the global goal (e.g., the agent wants to leave the room);
                                                                                                           medium frequency, path planning with waypoints that the
                                                                                                           agent will loosely follow to reach the goal (e.g., the path to
                                                                                                           reach the door and leave); high-frequency, steering where
                                                                                                           the agent is allowed to deviate from the path (e.g., to avoid
                                                                                                           collisions with other agents). Using these three stages, we
                                                                                                           can observe global emergent phenomena; for instance, evac-
                                                                                                           uation, where all agents must quickly leave an environment
                                                                                                           and navigate through bottlenecks. In the simulation, the sin-
                                                                                                           gle agent's behaviour influences the full crowd movement
                                                                                                           and leads to bottlenecks in the presence of small openings.

                                                                                                           by social interaction with other humans, personal motiva-
